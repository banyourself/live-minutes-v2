# Data Protection Agreement for Live Minutes (template)

Status: template for district counsel. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.
Fill in the bracketed parts, and replace this document with the district's standard agreement if the
district has one (for example the California Student Privacy Alliance agreement).

This agreement is between [District name] ("District") and Kevin Le, doing business as Live Minutes
("Provider"), and covers the Live Minutes service the District uses at [colleges].

## 1. Purpose and school official status

1.1 Provider supplies a service that turns meeting captions, transcripts, and chat into draft minutes
for the District's student governments, clubs, and committees, which District users review and approve.

1.2 For any education records it receives, Provider acts as a school official with a legitimate
educational interest under FERPA (34 CFR 99.31(a)(1)), performs a service the District would otherwise
perform itself, and is under the District's direct control for the use and maintenance of those records.

1.3 Provider uses District data only to provide and support the service under this agreement.

## 2. Data covered

2.1 "District Data" means everything District users put into or create with the service: account names,
email addresses, account types, confirmed school emails, organization memberships and officer positions,
meeting transcripts, chat, notes, drafts and approved minutes, motions and votes, funding requests,
translations, summaries, and activity logs.

2.2 The District owns District Data. Provider gains no ownership or license to it beyond what this
agreement needs.

## 3. Use and disclosure

3.1 Provider will not sell District Data, use it for advertising, build profiles of students for any
purpose other than the service, or use it to train AI models.

3.2 Provider will not disclose District Data except to the subprocessors listed in the attached
subprocessor list, to District users the District has authorized, or as required by law after notice to
the District where allowed.

3.3 AI processing: meeting text is sent to the AI provider chosen inside the service by the District, a
college, an organization, or a user. The District may require that only District-approved AI providers
are used by turning off personal AI keys. The District is responsible for its agreements with any AI
provider it connects, including retention and training terms.

## 4. Security

4.1 Provider will maintain the safeguards described in the HECVAT Lite answers, including encrypted
connections, encrypted stored credentials, hashed tokens, role-based access, audit logging, and
automated security scanning of each release.

4.2 Provider will limit its own staff access to District Data to what is needed to support the service.

## 5. Breach notification

5.1 Provider will notify the District contact without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours after
confirming unauthorized access to District Data, with what happened, what data was involved, the steps
taken, and a contact for questions. Provider will cooperate with the District's investigation and with
any notices the District must send.

## 6. Access, correction, and export

6.1 The District can export all of its data at any time from the IT console as one ZIP file.

6.2 Provider will help the District respond to requests from students or parents to inspect, correct, or
delete records, within [10] business days of the District's request.

## 7. Retention and deletion

7.1 The District sets retention periods in the service. Records under a District legal hold are not
deleted.

7.2 When this agreement ends, Provider will give the District a final export on request and then delete
District Data within [60] days, including backups within [90] days, and confirm deletion in writing.
If the District runs its own deployment, the District controls deletion directly.

## 8. Hosting

8.1 [Choose one] The District hosts the service in its own cloud subscription or data center, and
Provider has access only as the District grants it. / Provider hosts the service in [location] for the
District under this agreement.

## 9. Term

9.1 This agreement runs from [start date] until the District stops using the service and Provider has
completed the deletion in section 7.
