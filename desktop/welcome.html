      Welcome

      Minutes that write themselves while you meet

      Live Minutes reads the captions your meeting already has and drafts minutes in your organization's format as people talk. You review it, fix anything marked [verify], and approve.

      • ✎

        Drafts as you meet

        Every topic becomes one clear bullet, in your template.

      • ✓

        Tracks motions and votes

        Who moved, who seconded, and how the vote went.

      • ⇩

        Ready to share

        Download the Word file, or publish approved minutes if your group wants to.

      Live capture

      Bring Zoom captions in, right from this app

      1. Connect this computerPaste a capture token from Settings, Capture devices. It works only for your organization and you can revoke it.
      2. Pick the meetingChoose the Live Minutes meeting the captions belong to.
      3. Join Zoom herePaste the Zoom link, turn on captions, then click Pick captions area.

      A small floating bar shows that capture is live, how many lines were sent, and opens the draft in one click. Let everyone in the meeting know captions are being captured for minutes.

      Get started

      Sign in to start your first meeting

      Use the same account as the website. New here? Create a free account, choose Personal for your own meetings or School to join your organization.

      • Live Minutes reads only the caption text Zoom shows in this app. It never records audio or video.
      • Your capture token is stored encrypted on this computer.
      • Nothing about your meetings is public unless your organization publishes approved minutes.
      Server

      Only change this if your college or district runs its own copy of Live Minutes. Leave it empty to use minutes.kevinle.tech.

      For security, this app opens only your server, Zoom, Microsoft, and Google. If your school's Microsoft or Google sign-in sends you to its own login page, add that address here.
