## Short description (150 characters or fewer)

Turn your Zoom cloud recordings into draft meeting minutes. Imports the transcript, captions, and chat for a person to review and approve.

## Long description (2000 characters or fewer)

Live Minutes drafts meeting minutes for student governments, clubs, committees, and anyone who runs regular meetings. Connect the Zoom account that hosts your meetings, choose a cloud recording, and Live Minutes imports its transcript, closed captions, and chat so the AI you choose can draft the minutes in your organization's format. A person always reviews and approves the draft before it becomes official.

What the Zoom app does
- Lists your cloud recordings from the last 30 days so you can choose a meeting to import.
- Imports the recording's closed captions or audio transcript, plus the meeting chat.
- Shows a checklist of the Zoom recording settings it needs.
- Only reads. It cannot join, record, change, or delete anything in your Zoom account.

What you can do in Live Minutes
- Use your own minutes template, a list of topics, or one of seven built-in styles.
- Keep an official record of each motion: who moved, who seconded, the result, and each vote.
- See every version of the draft and restore any of them.
- Add a transcript from another note-taking app to double-check names, numbers, and votes.
- Export approved minutes as a Word file.

Your AI, your choice
Choose the AI provider, such as Claude, OpenAI, or Google Gemini, with your own key or one your school shares.

Privacy
- Meetings, transcripts, and minutes are private to your workspace.
- Your Zoom sign-in is stored encrypted. Disconnecting in Live Minutes, or removing the app in Zoom, deletes it right away.
- Live Minutes does not sell personal information, show ads, or use your records to train AI models.

Who can use it
Live Minutes needs an account, either in an organization approved by your college or district, or in a personal workspace by invitation. Importing requires Zoom cloud recording, which is part of paid Zoom plans.
